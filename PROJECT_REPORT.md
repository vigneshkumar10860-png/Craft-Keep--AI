# Project Report: CraftKeep
## AI for Transferring Traditional Craft Knowledge to the Next Generation

### 1. Introduction
Traditional crafts contain practical skills, cultural stories and community knowledge. Much of this knowledge is passed from experienced artisans to younger learners through observation and practice. When fewer young people have time or access to learn directly from artisans, some skills and stories may be lost. Digital tools and carefully supervised AI can help document and share this knowledge.

### 2. Problem statement
**How can AI help transfer traditional craft knowledge to the next generation?**

### 3. Objective
To design an accessible digital platform that helps artisans document traditional craft processes and helps students and young learners discover, understand and practise those skills.

### 4. Proposed solution
CraftKeep is a website prototype containing a searchable craft library, introductory step-by-step guides, audio narration and a form for suggesting craft stories. A future AI-enabled version could transcribe artisan interviews, create draft instructions, translate them into local languages and generate learning activities. All generated information must be reviewed by the relevant artisan or community.

### 5. Target audience
- Traditional artisans and craft practitioners
- School and college students
- Teachers and cultural educators
- Community organizations and museums
- People interested in local history and handmade products

### 6. System workflow
1. An artisan agrees to share their knowledge.
2. The craft process is recorded as text, photographs, audio or video.
3. AI tools can help transcribe, organize, summarize and translate the material.
4. The artisan reviews the result and corrects mistakes.
5. Approved guides are added to the craft library with appropriate credit.
6. Learners search, read, listen and practise under suitable guidance.

### 7. Technology used in this prototype
- HTML for the website structure
- CSS for layout, responsive design and visual presentation
- JavaScript for search, filtering, interactive guides and form handling
- Browser Speech Synthesis API for optional audio narration

The current project is a front-end demonstration. It does not send information to an AI model or save submissions to a server.

### 8. Benefits and social impact
- Preserves craft knowledge and oral histories in digital form
- Makes introductory learning resources easier to access
- Supports learning in audio-friendly formats
- Can make knowledge available in local languages
- Encourages younger generations to explore traditional skills
- Creates opportunities for artisans to be acknowledged as knowledge holders

### 9. Limitations
- Sample craft content is illustrative and requires review by practitioners.
- The demo has no database, login, cloud storage or real AI integration.
- Audio depends on browser and device support.
- Translation and transcription quality can vary by language and accent.
- Some traditional knowledge should not be shared publicly.

### 10. Responsible use of AI
The platform should obtain consent before recording, respect cultural ownership, credit knowledge holders, protect private information, and allow artisans to correct or remove content. AI-generated text must be reviewed before publication. AI should support artisans rather than replace their expertise.

### 11. Future scope
Future development may include Tamil-language support, offline access, secure cloud storage, AI transcription and translation, artisan profiles, video demonstrations, quizzes, and a community review process.

### 12. Conclusion
CraftKeep demonstrates how a simple digital platform can support the transfer of traditional craft knowledge. With consent, artisan review and responsible AI use, technology can help younger generations learn about traditional skills while respecting the communities that preserve them.
