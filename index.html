HOW CRAFTKEEP HELPS

From an elder’s hands to a learner’s screen.

AI can help organize and explain knowledge, but the artisan remains the expert and owner of the tradition.

01

Record the process

Capture photos, videos, tools, materials and the artisan’s own explanation.

02

Make it easier to learn

AI can draft clear steps, simplify difficult terms and translate descriptions into local languages for review.

03

Learn at your own pace

Use searchable guides and browser audio to listen while practising each step.
